# Areas

Ongoing lanes of responsibility that are not single projects. Unlike projects,
areas do not "finish"; they are maintained.

This folder ships empty on purpose. Areas are the most personal layer of the
vault: they describe how *you* carve up your work, and a borrowed set of lanes is
worse than none. Add them as they become obvious from the projects you actually
track, not before.

Create from `_templates/area.md`. An area note is a thin wrapper: a sentence on
what the lane is, wikilinks to the projects inside it, and any standing lesson
that applies across the whole lane.

Examples of what a lane looks like: a work domain, a technology you keep
returning to, a recurring kind of deliverable. If a lane would contain exactly
one project, it is not a lane, it is that project.
